The House Came With a Dock

If you just bought a house on the water, or you are about to, the first question is what will fit behind it.

There are three things we have to know before we can recommend a boat that will fit behind your new house.

  1. How long is the dock?
  2. How deep is the water at the dock?
  3. Are there any bridges between your dock and the inlet, and how much clearance?

Send me the address and I'll help you figure out what is possible at the house you own. Give me twenty minutes on the phone, or I'll come sit with you at the house. No questionnaire, no pressure.

Then we figure out what you need a boat to do, and find the one that does it and fits behind your house.
