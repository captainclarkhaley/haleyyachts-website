Miss Dunia

2008 Hargrave 101 Raised Pilothouse · $2,345,000

 

Every 100-footer on the market is described as well maintained. MISS DUNIA actually is. She is a 2008 Hargrave 101 Raised Pilothouse, run by a full-time crew for an owner who keeps her appearance and her systems at their best at all times. She is turnkey, actively managed, and ready for the next cruising season.

Step inside and the first thing you notice is the woodwork. Hargrave's joinery here is museum quality: high-gloss sapele throughout, and curved doorway frames in rare tiger wood. Every guest stateroom has its own ensuite in granite and marble, and the master has a four-poster king and a his-and-hers bath with a Jacuzzi tub to port and a shower to starboard.

There are four guest staterooms: the master, a forward VIP, an aft VIP with a queen, and a twin cabin that converts to a queen, with a Pullman. The galley is Hargrave's signature country kitchen, with Green Butterfly granite and a dinette for six. Crew live separately, with a captain's cabin and private head plus two crew cabins.

Outside, she is built for entertaining. The aft deck seats 8 to 10 around a teak table, with a wet bar and a drop-down 42-inch TV. Up top, the hardtop flybridge has a built-in hot tub, a wet bar with four stools, two large settees and a grill. A Nautica RIB17 Widebody tender with a 90hp Yamaha rides aboard, handled by a 2,000 lb hydraulic crane.

The work is already done

  • Drydock October/November 2024: bottom paint, Propspeed, props rebalanced, stabilizer fins dropped, reglassed and painted
  • New energy-efficient Termodinamica A/C throughout, flybridge included: new chillers, 12 air handlers and pumps
  • Port generator replaced with a new 45kW John Deere / Marathon unit, now at 450 hours
  • Starlink, Wi-Fi security cameras, and a cellular pump and bilge alarm
  • New TVs throughout (2023 to 2025), new aft deck and bow cushions, all batteries replaced 2022
  • Life rafts and fire system recertified 2024; hull and house detailed, buffed and waxed March 2026

Twin Caterpillar C32s at 1,675 hp each cruise her at 15 knots with 21 on tap. She carries 3,800 gallons of fuel and 800 of fresh water, with an 800 GPD Bluewater watermaker and Naiad stabilizers.
