Brilliant By Design

The New Riviera 645 SUV Series II

 

When Riviera launched the original 645 SUV back in 2021, it delivered on a simple promise: freedom and adventure on a grand scale. A new generation has now arrived, and I think it is worth your attention. The Riviera 645 SUV Series II is the result of a thorough rethink of the interior, with a fresh design that reads contemporary and timeless at the same time. In a line, it is more functional, more luxurious, and more enjoyable.

What I love about the SUV concept is that you really are getting two spectacular yachts in one. She marries the proven blue-water performance and wide-open cockpit of Riviera's Sport Motor Yachts with the single-level alfresco entertaining of their award-winning Sport Yachts. Big water sports cockpit aft, single-level luxury flowing from the alfresco deck through the saloon, and a generous accommodation deck below with a choice of three or four staterooms, three bathrooms, and a full-beam master suite.

What is new in Series II

  • Redesigned galley with a full-height fridge and a wet bar worked into the bench, so you provision once and stay out longer
  • Diagonally opposing saloon lounges, plus a forward companion lounge opposite the helm
  • A 55-inch TV on an electric lifter, integrated CZone cabinet with storage and phone charging, and soundbars in the master and VIP staterooms
  • A new starboard helm with dual chairs, a larger starboard stateroom, and centre companionway stairs
  • Anchor, aft deck, twin engine room, and cockpit awning cameras for single-glance awareness
  • Stern thruster and EPS included, with an available MAN 1550 engine option

The helm is state of the art: touch-screen navigation, outstanding visibility through the curved glass windscreen, and a joystick at both the helm and the cockpit that makes docking a 64-footer feel easy. For the anglers, there is a full sport-fishing package available, from a transom live-bait tank and outriggers to heavy-duty rod holders and an insulated fish bin.

She is also a genuinely smart boat. Sentinel telematics let you monitor and control systems remotely, and options run to Humphree fins or Seakeeper stabilization, Praxis composite solar charging, and Starlink. Every Riviera is backed by their two-year express and seven-year structural limited warranties, from a builder that has launched more than 6,300 yachts over 46 years.

This is the boat I point people to when they want one yacht that does it all: fish it, cruise it, and entertain on it, without stepping up to a full crew.

The new-generation foredeck is a second living room in the open air: a redesigned table and comfortable lounging, and the best seat aboard for a sunset at anchor.
