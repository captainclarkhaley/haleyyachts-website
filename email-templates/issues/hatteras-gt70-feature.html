Hatteras GT70

2024 Hatteras GT70 · $6,700,000

 

She can run with the big dogs in the rough stuff. This is a 2024 Hatteras GT70, near new, with under 200 hours on her mains, debugged and ready to go.

The GT70 is built with a resin-infused hull for a good strength-to-weight ratio, and her variable dead-rise hull form has a sharp entry for knifing through seas. With Caterpillar C-32 B power, CJR props and twisted rudders, she can run at nearly 40 knots on the pins and is comfortable cruising at 34.

Her large cockpit, 205 sq ft, with an air-conditioned mezzanine and SeaKeeper stabilization, offers you a first-class boating and fishing experience. A BlueWater fighting chair sits in the middle, with fish boxes plumbed for chipped ice, a windowed transom live well and a tackle center close at hand.

Up top, the flybridge helm carries three flush-mounted 24-inch Garmin touchscreens and a Furuno Omni sonar. Inside, there are five staterooms and four heads, finished in high-gloss mahogany joinery with granite counters in the galley and heads.
