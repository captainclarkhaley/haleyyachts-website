At 1.350″ wide the One Water mark is 0.350″ tall. The finest features in the artwork are the radiating lines in the compass ring, which measure 0.45 of the mark's 90 units, i.e. 0.5% of its height. At 0.350″ tall that is 0.0017″, about 0.126pt. Offset wants roughly 0.25pt minimum on coated and 0.5pt on uncoated, so the ring detail is about four times below the floor. It will not print as drawn: the ring will fill to a flat pale disc, and on uncoated it will plug. For those lines to reach 0.25pt the mark would have to run 2.68″ wide, which is twice the space the corner has and would put One Water's mark above Clark's in the hierarchy. There is no in-between.
